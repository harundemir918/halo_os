WHAT CHANGES
Signal splits in three
Volt is too bright to read on paper, so lines use Moss and text uses Olive. Volt stays as a fill with an ink edge.
Switches invert
On is an ink track with a Volt knob, so it stays obvious on a light ground.
Edges do the work
Hairlines go from white 8% to ink 12%. Still no blur, gradients or shadows.
